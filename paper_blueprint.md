## 1. The Paper in One Paragraph

> We introduce a controlled experimental instrument — *item families* in which one abstract argument structure (agent–predicate–patient) is realized across multiple surface forms, and camouflaged pairs in which matched surface frames realize different structures — and use it to measure, within a single design, how surface-form sensitivity declines and structure sensitivity rises across transformer depth. We characterize each model's **invariance transition** (the depth at which structure overtakes surface), its trajectory shape, its geometric signature (intrinsic dimension, neighborhood overlap, alignment), and the preservation of structural relations through depth. We then link, *item by item*, the measured depth-wise invariance to behavioral generalization under held-out surface forms, and test whether the transition profile is conserved across six models spanning two architecture families and a random-init control. Results bridge two literatures that currently do not speak: representation-emergence (when/where) and behavioral generalization (does it survive surface change) — and establish the phenomenological targets for the causal-mechanism questions that remain open.

## 4. Locked Design Decisions (defaults — edit before Week 1 if you disagree)

- **D1 · Domain:** Argument structure / semantic roles with classical alternations. Families: *same roles, different surface* → active, passive, dative-NP-PP, dative-NP-NP (4 forms × k families). *Same surface frame, different roles* → camouflaged role-reversal pairs ("The chef praised the waiter" ↔ "The waiter praised the chef") + attachment-ambiguous controls. This matches your agent–predicate–patient framing and sits exactly between the TACL generalization paper and the structural-probe lineage.
- **D2 · Model panel (fits 4 GB, spans axes):** `bert-base-uncased`, `roberta-base`, `distilbert-base-uncased`, `gpt2` (124 M), `EleutherAI/pythia-410m`, + **`bert-base` random-init control** (architecture-without-pretraining — the cheapest, strongest control in the paper). All frozen. Encoder models for role-token representations; decoder models get the same treatment with left-context states.
- **D3 · Representation unit:** the **argument tokens themselves** (agent/patient head tokens), not [CLS]. Pool predicate tokens separately. ← keeps storage tiny (see §5) and is methodologically correct for roles.
- **D4 · Behavioral assay (RQ5):** fine-tune a token-classification head (ARG0/ARG1/PRED) on **one surface form only** (active), evaluate on held-out forms (passive, dative) with **filler-disjoint** train/test. Per-item success × per-item invariance margin at l* → logistic model + rank correlation. Full fine-tune on `distilbert` + `gpt2` only (the two fastest).
- **D5 · Statistics:** cluster bootstrap over item families (95% CIs), permutation tests for l* differences, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR across the 12–24 layer-wise tests. Report effect sizes. No p-value theater.
- **D6 · Venue ladder:** **Primary: Cognitive Computation (Q1, SJR 1.197, IF ~7.4–8.6 — best scope fit: cognitive systems + computational accounts)** → **Neurocomputing (Q1, SJR 1.471, IF ~6.5)** as second Q1 shot → fallback **Applied Intelligence (Q2, SJR 0.889)** or **Computer Speech & Language (Q2, SJR 0.702)**. All verified Scopus Q1/Q2 as of 2024–2026 SJR.

## 5. The Instrument (C1) — Dataset Spec

```
FAMILY = abstract structure: AGENT(fill) PREDICATE(fill) PATIENT(fill)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Forms (same structure, different surface):
  F1 active     "Nina praised the editor."
  F2 passive    "The editor was praised by Nina."
  F3 dative-PP  "Omar gave the book to the clerk."
  F4 dative-NP  "Omar gave the clerk the book."

CAMOUFLAGE (same surface frame, different structure):
  C1 role swap  "The chef praised the waiter." / "The waiter praised the chef."
  C2 attachment "The spy saw the man with the binoculars." (2 parses)
```

- **Size:** ~800 families × 4 forms ≈ 3,200 sentences + ~1,600 camouflaged/controls + ~200 naturalistic validation sentences (drawn from a corpus, human-checked — pre-empts "synthetic only" attack). Total ≈ 5,000 items.
- **Fillers:** frequency-matched noun/verb lists; **all splits disjoint on fillers** (forces true generalization, TACL-style novel-verb logic).
- **Generator:** `data/generate_dataset.py` → `items.csv` (family_id, form, structure_label, surface_label, tokens, role_spans). Script-generated, version-controlled, released with the paper.
- **Storage math (why 4 GB VRAM / 16 GB RAM is fine):** role-token states only: 6 tokens × 12 layers × 768 dims × fp16 ≈ 110 KB/item → 5,000 items ≈ 0.55 GB **per model** → ~3.5 GB on disk for the whole panel. Analysis loads layer-slices lazily.

## 6. RQ Operationalization (the analysis contract)

| RQ | Construct | Primary metric | Controls | Analysis | Figure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RQ1** *When?* | Invariance transition layer l* | **S_l** = within-family distance across surface variants (surface sensitivity ↓ expected); **T_l** = between-structure separability (probe accuracy / Fisher ratio ↑ expected); l* = first depth where T_l > S_l sustained k layers | Random-label probes → **selectivity**; MDL; random-init model; lexical-content baseline (bag-of-words) | Curves with cluster-bootstrap CIs per model | **F1** (S_l, T_l, l* per model) |
| **RQ2** *Trajectory?* | Shape of development | Segmented regression / changepoint on S_l, T_l; typology: monotone vs encode–reconstruct (cf. Voita) vs two-stage; stage ordering across models | Same + length/frequency covariates | Model comparison (1-break vs 2-break fits, BIC) | **F2** (trajectory typology + stage alignment) |
| **RQ3** *Geometry?* | Shape of the transition | TwoNN intrinsic dimension per layer; neighborhood overlap χ(l, l+1); CKA across layers **split by condition** (surface-variant sets vs structure-same sets) | ID estimator sanity (random projection); batch/precision check | ID peak position vs l* (prediction: geometry reorganizes *before* l*; Valeriani-style) | **F3** (ID + χ + condition-split CKA) |
| **RQ4** *Structure preserved?* | Relational fidelity through depth | Within-family representation-distance matrix vs structural-distance matrix (Mantel-style correlation per layer); probe **retention/leakage** across layers | Control tasks (Hewitt–Liang); filler-only baselines | Correlation profiles; preservation index from l* onward | **F4** (preservation profile) |
| **RQ5** *Generalizes?* | **Link:** invariance ↔ behavior | Per-item **invariance margin** m_i = T_{l*}(i) − S_{l*}(i); behavioral y_i = cross-form role-labeling success after active-only fine-tune (D4) | Length, frequency, surprisal covariates; layer-shuffled margin as null | Logistic model y ~ m + covariates; rank correlation; ΔAUC of margin over probe-accuracy-only predictor | **F5** (margin → generalization; the headline panel) |
| **RQ6** *Architecture-general?* | Universality of the profile | Normalized-depth profiles S(τ), T(τ), τ = l/L; l*/L per model; profile correlation matrix; functional alignment (DTW) | Random-init control must *fail* the profile — if it doesn't, claim collapses honestly | Clustering of models by profile; agreement of stage order | **F6** (6-model profile overlay + alignment matrix) |

**Interpretation guardrail (write this into the paper, §6/Discussion):** these are *phenomenological* results — they localize and characterize the transition and establish behavioral stakes. They do **not** claim circuit-level mechanism; they define the intervention windows that a follow-up (RQ7, causal patching) should target. This is the honest boundary the literature map demands — state it first, and the "but probing isn't mechanism" review becomes a *conclusion you already drew*.

## 9. Reviewer Preemption (the four attacks you already know are coming)

| Attack | Defense (already designed in) |
|---|---|
| "Sophisticated Tenney et al. 2019 — we already know *when* syntax appears" | We measure **invariance**, not decodability: S_l/T_l crossover on same-structure/different-surface families; selectivity-controlled; and we link it to behavior (C5) — Tenney has no behavioral link. |
| "Probing ≠ mechanism" | We *state* the phenomenology→mechanism boundary ourselves (§6) and frame results as localized targets for causal follow-up. The paper's claim ends where mechanism begins — deliberately. |
| "Synthetic templates → no ecological validity" | Control design is the point (identical fillers across forms); plus the 200-item naturalistic validation set replicates the l* finding on corpus sentences. |
| "Laptop-scale models only" | Claims are about *profile conservation across families/scales*, not frontier-model behavior; random-init control isolates pretraining; panel spans encoder/decoder families (D2). |

**Cut-order if behind schedule:** (1) cleft/topicalization forms → 3 forms per family; (2) one model from the panel (keep random-init); (3) neighborhood overlap χ → keep ID + CKA; (4) DTW alignment → plain profile correlation. **Never cut:** the random-init control, selectivity controls, and RQ5.
