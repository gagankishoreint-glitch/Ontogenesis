# Measurement Specification — Computational Ontogenesis

**Status: FROZEN at commit `24166f57ab7e5fe5cc9417a72a9165e058231016` (2026-10-09).**
This document records the measurement specification exactly as implemented and
verified at that commit. The Phase-1 naturalistic retrieval repair (2026-10-09,
later commits) does **not** alter anything in this spec.

**Change control.** Do not change model measurements, \(l^*\), \(l_{\text{struct}}\),
dataset partitions, behavioral metrics, scientific claims, or the
observer/annotation protocol to accommodate a retrieval problem. Any change to
this spec requires a new versioned spec document and PI sign-off; the frozen
commit remains the reference point for every reported number.

**Deferred questions.** RQ3 (geometry) and RQ4 (structure preservation) are
deferred and are not part of the core paper claims. The core paper investigates
RQ1 (where the structural transition emerges), RQ6 (recurrence across the
tested architectures), and RQ5 (margin→behavior link). The naturalistic arm
(N1) is a **validation** of those three claims, not a fourth research question.

regenerable). Verified counts (2026-10-08, re-verified 2026-10-09):

| quantity | value |
|---|---|

| splits | P1 1,900 / P2 1,900 / aux 60 |
| structures | trans 3,000 / dat 800 / ambig 60 |

- **Family:** one abstract argument structure (agent–predicate–patient) realized
  across multiple surface forms; camouflaged pairs realize different structures
  in matched surface frames.
- **Splits are filler-disjoint** (P1/P2 share no fillers) — forces true
  generalization rather than lexical recognition.
- **Representation unit:** the argument tokens themselves (agent/patient head
  tokens), slots 0–3; pooled representation = mean of the first three slots
  for the geometry curves; probe features = concatenation of the noun1/noun2
  span states.
- `MAX_LEN = 64` (subword safety margin); `EXTRACT_BATCH = 4`.

## 3. Metrics — `ontogenesis/src/invariance.py` (v3)

Layer indexing: `l = 0` is the embedding layer and is **excluded from all
significance inference**; layers run `1..L-1` (reported `n_layers = L`, e.g.
13 for 12-layer models).

| metric | definition |
|---|---|
| \(W_l\) (\(S_l\), surface sensitivity) | mean within-family cosine distance across the 4 surface forms (active, passive, cleft, topical; `TRANS_FORMS_S`), cluster-bootstrap 95% CI over families. May rise with depth — informative either way. |
| \(B_l\) | between-family, form-matched cosine distance |
| \(R_l\) | relative surface sensitivity \(W_l / B_l\); falling \(R_l\) = structural organization strengthening despite depth |
| \(T_l\) (sentence-level structure decoding) | logistic probe on `[noun1; noun2]` predicting `label_patient_first`. Train: P1 {active, passive}; in-form test: P2 {active, passive}; **cross-form test: P2 {cleft, topical}**. Shuffled-label selectivity control; binomial test per layer + BH-FDR q < 0.05 across layers. |
| \(l_{\text{struct}}\) | **first layer** with cross-form \(T_l\) significant (q < 0.05) and accuracy > 0.5 — the *sentence-level probe* transition. |
| span probe (v3) | \(x\) = one span's hidden state; \(y = 1\) iff that span is the AGENT. Labels are balanced within every construction, so cross-form accuracy requires role marking, not construction recognition. Train on individual spans (P1 {active, passive}); evaluate **pairwise per sentence** (agent-span logit > patient-span logit; one Bernoulli per sentence, chance 0.5). Same binomial + BH-FDR procedure. |
| \(l^*\) ≡ `l_struct_span` | **first layer** with span-probe cross-form pairwise accuracy significant (q < 0.05, acc > 0.5) — the *span-level AGENT-probe* transition. **RQ5 uses \(l^*\) = `l_struct_span`.** |
| \(l_{\text{surf}}\) | informational half-drop layer of \(W_l\) (not a transition claim) |
| G1 gate | `go_no_go = (max cross-form acc > 0.7) and (l_struct is not None)`; span gate `go_no_go_span` analogously with `l_struct_span` |

**\(l^*\) vs \(l_{\text{struct}}\) — do not conflate.** \(l_{\text{struct}}\) is the
transition of the sentence-level `[noun1; noun2] → label_patient_first` probe;
\(l^*\) (`l_struct_span`) is the transition of the span-level AGENT probe with
construction-balanced labels. Both are reported per model
(`results/{model}_summary.json`); the RQ5 margin is computed at \(l^*\)
(`l_struct_span`). Verified values (2026-10-08): distilbert
\(l_{\text{struct}}=3\), \(l^*=3\); gpt2 \(l_{\text{struct}}=5\), \(l^*=4\);
bert_base \(l_{\text{struct}}=6\) (span not reported in its summary);
bert_random: both `null`, both gates NO-GO.

## 4. RQ5 behavioral assay — `ontogenesis/src/behavior.py` (v3, anchored)

- Each sentence yields **two** examples, one per query order: head input
  `[h_u; h_v]`, label \(y = 1\) iff queried \(u\) is the AGENT. Every
  construction contains both labels, so construction identity is uninformative
  (fixes the v1 flaw where label was a deterministic function of form).
- Train: P1 {active, passive} × 2 orders = 1,200 (balanced per form). Select:
  best epoch on in-form P2. Evaluate: P2 {active, passive, cleft, topical} × 2
  orders = 2,400 graded \(p_{correct}\). Per-form **mean and std** of
  \(p_{correct}\) are reported (within-form std is the variance RQ5 needs).
- **Per-item structural margin** (`ontogenesis/src/rq5_link.py` v3.1), at
  \(l^*\) = `l_struct_span`:
  \(\text{margin}_s = (2y-1)\,[g_l(h_u) - g_l(h_v)]\), with \(g_l\) the frozen
  span-agent probe. The margin is identical for the two query orders of a
  sentence, so analyses aggregate to sentence level (n = 600 cross-form
  sentences; \(p_{correct}\) = mean over the two orders).
- **Estimand:** within-form Spearman \(\rho\) (mean of per-form \(\rho\) over
  the two role-forms). **Pooled \(\rho\) is form-confounded and is reported but
  never used as the headline estimand.**

## 5. Controls (all frozen)

- Shuffled-label probes ≈ chance at all layers (selectivity).
- `bert_random` (random-init): if \(l^*\) appears without pretraining, the
  phenomenon is an artifact — it does not (NO-GO on both levels).
- Position-only baseline at chance; window bag-of-words probe collapses under
  the filler-disjoint split (margin is not lexical); MLP readout does not
  exceed the linear span probe (no linear-probe artifact).
- Layer-0 (embeddings) excluded from FDR; BH-FDR across layers.

## 6. Naturalistic arm (N1) — validation of RQ1/RQ6/RQ5 on attested sentences

- **Instruments are frozen**: the span probe and the template-trained behavior
  head are applied **zero-shot** to human-verified attested sentences
  (`data/naturalistic_items.csv`, exported from the annotation journal by
  `data/annotate.py --export`). No retraining, no hyperparameter adjustment on
  natural data, ever.
- **Form set (v2 decision, swap_passive, 2026-10-08):** cleft (it-cleft, NP
  focus; cross-form headline replication), passive (by-phrase; role reversal
  under style shift), canonical (unmarked SVO; instrument-sanity anchor).
  Topicalization is **not** in the form set (attested NP-fronting < 0.2 % of
  screened sentences; reported as a limitation).
- **Pipeline:** T3a `src/extract.py --items data/naturalistic_items.csv`;
  T3b `src/behavior.py --eval-external`; T3c `src/rq5_link.py --natural`
  → `results/{model}_rq5_natural.json` + `figures/rq5_{model}_natural.png`.
- **Decision rules (pre-register as N1 before the first run):** R1 primary
  (mean-within-form \(\rho > 0\), q < 0.05 BH over forms, for distilbert and
  gpt2); R2 magnitude (Fisher-z CI vs template \(\rho\)); R3 instrument gate
  on natural canonical sentences (behavior \(p_s\) std ≥ 0.02 and span-probe
  pairwise accuracy ≥ 0.65); R4 `lexicon_overlap` vocabulary split; R5
  co-annotator agreement reporting.
- **Annotation gates:** target 60 accepted sentences per form (180 total);
  preregistered minimum floor 55 per form (165 total). The floor may be invoked
  only if corpus availability genuinely prevents reaching the target, with the
  shortfall documented **before** computing any naturalistic margin–behavior
  correlation. Export gates: offsets resolve, all spans tokenize under
  `MAX_LEN`, no duplicate texts, ≥ min-per-form keeps (`annotate.py --export`
  default `--min-per-form 55`). QC: blind re-annotation of 20 % of keeps after
  ≥ 48 h; agreement < 95 % ⇒ full re-review of the affected form.
